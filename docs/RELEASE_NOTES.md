# ASTRA 2.0 — NASA Space Apps Challenge Release

ASTRA 2.0 brings together a local spacecraft mission console, a 90-day fictional dataset, and personalized longitudinal monitoring. Start at Day 45, advance replay, and trace a finding back to the relevant personal reference, observations, and data quality.

- Native statistical monitoring computes personal references, trends, and contextual findings from the published replay snapshot.
- Data & Science documents scientific sources, methods, verification, and the team's use of ChatGPT and Codex. The six views share the atmospheric redesign and accessible selector styling.
- Research Prototypes includes synthetic facial and speech feature demos and separate optional Windows capture integrations.
- ASTRA Companion offers recreational conversation through the optional local Qwen model.

See the [README](../README.md) for Windows setup and the separate optional dependencies. ASTRA uses synthetic data and has no clinical or flight validation.
